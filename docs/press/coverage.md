# Press coverage

A running log of where Submarius has been mentioned, reviewed, or
covered by independent media. Updated as new coverage appears.

If we missed your piece, please [open an issue](https://github.com/Submarius/submarius/issues)
or email us via the contact link on [submarius.com](https://submarius.com).

## Format

Each entry: **date** — **publication** — *headline / link* — short
characterisation.

## How we treat coverage

- **Listed regardless of slant.** Critical reviews, mixed reviews, and
  glowing reviews all get listed. The point of this page is to be a
  navigation aid, not a marketing surface.
- **Linked to the original publication**, not to a syndicated copy or
  an aggregator.
- **Excerpted briefly** so the reader can decide whether to click. We
  don't republish full articles.
- **Removed only on request** from the original publication, never on
  request from us when the coverage was unfavourable.

## How to cover Submarius

The [press kit](press-kit.md) has logos, boilerplate, screenshots,
and quick facts. Free to use under CC BY 4.0.

For interviews, fact-checking, or anything bespoke, the contact link
on [submarius.com](https://submarius.com) is the fastest route.
