# Pro vs Free

Submarius has a free tier and a paid Pro tier. The line between them is
drawn deliberately: **safety features are free regardless of payment**;
forecast depth and intelligence layers are Pro.

## Free, forever

These features are available to every user, with no time limit and no
"upgrade to continue" prompts:

- **SOS** with Plus Code rescue location
- **Dive-buddy GPS sharing**
- **OCEARCH tagged-shark proximity** alerts
- **Crowd-sourced shark sightings**
- **Tide predictions** for any coastal location
- **Sun and moon times**
- **The current marine forecast** (today and tomorrow)
- **Today's water-clarity estimate** (with band)
- **Today's GO/NO verdict**
- **Spot library** (private, buddy-shared, or public-fuzzed) with
  end-to-end encrypted backup
- **Catch logging**
- **Offline base map** (bathymetry + structure)
- **Reefs and wrecks overlay**

In other words: the safety net, the basic forecast, your personal data,
and offline operation. The free tier is a complete usable product, not
a teaser.

## Pro

Pricing: **$12.99/month**, **$99/year**, or **$149 lifetime** (current
prices; check the app for live values).

What you get on top of Free:

- **7-day forecast** (vs today and tomorrow)
- **Hourly granularity** for the full window
- **Water-clarity forecast** for the full 7-day horizon, including
  best-window detection ("best dive window this week: Saturday 9 AM–
  noon, ~12 m visibility")
- **Bite-score forecast** for the full horizon, per-species
- **Animated precipitation radar** overlay
- **Sea-surface temperature** overlay with thermocline detection
- **Wind-particle** map
- **HRRR short-range wind raster**
- **Multi-spot trip planner**
- **Push notifications** for verdict changes at saved spots
- **Higher upload quota** for catch and viz reports

Pro is the unit-economics-positive subscription that funds the data
ingest pipelines (CoastWatch, GOES-16 ABI, USGS, OCEARCH, etc.) and the
ongoing work on the model. The free tier is genuinely free, not
loss-led — its scope is sized to be servable with the Pro revenue.

## Why this split

Three commitments, in order of priority:

1. **Safety features must reach everyone.** A locked SOS button is
   wrong, full stop. Same for buddy-GPS and shark proximity.
2. **Today's reality should be visible to everyone.** If conditions are
   dangerous *right now*, you shouldn't need a subscription to see
   that. Today's verdict is free.
3. **Forecast depth, time horizon, and intelligence layers are
   Pro.** The 7-day clarity forecast is the active product — what
   spearos pay for, what justifies the subscription. The free tier
   doesn't include it.

The split holds even when it costs us conversion. We've been asked to
gate buddy-GPS or to limit SOS to one trigger per month. We won't.

## Lifetime tier

The $149 lifetime option exists for users who'd rather pay once than
think about a recurring bill. It's a real lifetime — you keep Pro for
as long as Submarius runs, with no future upsell. We may raise the
lifetime price for new buyers as the product grows; existing lifetime
users are grandfathered.

## Cancellation

Subscriptions are managed through the Apple App Store. Cancel anytime
in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. There's no in-app dark pattern
to make cancellation hard.
